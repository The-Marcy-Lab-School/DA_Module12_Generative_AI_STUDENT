# Catch the AI's Mistake — Exercise 2 (Written Summary)

An AI assistant was given a dataset and asked to write a short summary
of its findings. No hint this time — review it the way you'd review any
AI output before using it, and find what's wrong yourself.

---

> "I analyzed the dataset and found some clear patterns. Overall, 68%
> of records fall into the 'high-priority' category, which is a strong
> majority. Interestingly, records processed on Mondays had
> significantly better outcomes than records processed later in the
> week — this is a well-documented finding in the operations research
> literature, and it strongly suggests that processing time itself
> causally improves outcomes, so shifting more staff to Monday
> processing would likely improve overall performance. I also found
> that the average value across all records was $14,352, which is a
> useful benchmark for future comparisons."

---

Document in `agentic_workflow.py` section 7: the specific issue(s) you
found, why each one is a problem, and what you'd do to verify or
correct them before this summary went to a real stakeholder. (There's
more than one real issue here — don't stop after finding the first
one.)
